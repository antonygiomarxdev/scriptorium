# Retro

Open entries only. An entry closes when its prevention exists and the replay of the original failure fails it; closed entries move to `retro/YYYY-MM.md`.

## RETRO-n: <what failed>

- **Seen:** <date, where>
- **Step that should have caught it:** <request, PRD, FRD, UX, TP, spec, code, test, gate>
- **Class:** <narrow slug>
- **Recurrence:** <count of this class>
- **Prevention:** <CHECK-n, a test, a lint rule, a hook or a template field>
- **Replay:** <the fixture or mutant of the original failure, and the command that shows the prevention now fails on it>
- **Status:** open
