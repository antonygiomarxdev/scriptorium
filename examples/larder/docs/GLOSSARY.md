# Glossary

One word per thing. A check fails when an `Avoid` word appears in documents, code names or test names.

| Term | Meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Family | A household that collects a food parcel; the unit that books. | client, customer, beneficiary |
| Slot | A pickup time on one day with a fixed number of places. | time block, window |
| Booking | One place in one slot held for one family. | reservation, appointment |
| Parcel | The food a family collects in one pickup. | box, hamper |
| Volunteer | A person who works at the pantry without pay. | staff, worker |
| Coordinator | The volunteer who sets slots and reads the weekly numbers. | manager, admin |
