An ADR file may be named NNNN-name.md as well as ADR-NNNN-name.md. A project that adopts the chain sets "legacyAdrsThrough" in .scriptorium/config.json: ADRs at or below it are not held to the ADR template or line cap, and a TP may cite one unless it says it is not accepted. The adopt skill sets it.
