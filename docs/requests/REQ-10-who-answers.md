# REQ-10: Ask the owner only what only the owner can answer

Status: Accepted

## Summary

Agents send the owner every open question, including facts a researcher can find and choices the lead can make and record. Every open question must say who answers it; the owner gets only the ones that need the owner, in one message, each with a recommendation.

Last change: 2026-11-20, opened and accepted

## Who asked and when
The owner, 2026-11-20.

## What they said
> I'd tell you these things can be researched. I don't feel it's necessary for me to give you 100% of things, because in the end they are topics that can be researched. (Translated from Spanish.) And, on this proposal: Go ahead, it looks good to me.

## The problem behind it
An open question is a bare Q-n line. Nothing records who should answer it, so an agent asks the owner by default, and the owner is the bottleneck for questions with sources.

## Evidence
On 2026-11-20, in another project using the chain, an agent asked the owner four questions before an approval; one was a domain fact with published sources, answered only after the owner said it could be researched.

## Decision
Accepted by the owner 2026-11-20 into the chain slice, D-36.
